# Dog Care Guidelines

General guidance for adult dogs. Puppies, seniors and dogs with medical conditions need individual advice from a vet.

## Dog walks and exercise
species: dog
topic: walk
recommended_minutes: 30-60
recommended_per_day: 2

Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of walking or active play each day, ideally split into two walks (morning and evening). High-energy breeds such as retrievers and border collies may need more. A walk shorter than 15 minutes is usually not enough exercise for an adult dog. Walks help with weight, behavior and mental stimulation.

## Dog feeding
species: dog
topic: feeding
recommended_minutes: 10-15
recommended_per_day: 2

Adult dogs are usually fed twice a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart, for example breakfast around 07:30 and dinner around 18:00. Feeding takes 10 to 15 minutes including fresh water. Avoid exercising hard right after a meal. Puppies need three to four small meals a day.

## Dog grooming and bathing
species: dog
topic: grooming
recommended_minutes: 15-30
recommended_per_day: 0

Brush dogs weekly, more often for long-haired breeds. Bathing is needed about once every one to three months unless the dog gets dirty. Grooming sessions take 15 to 30 minutes and are also a chance to check the skin, ears and nails for problems.

## Dog play and enrichment
species: dog
topic: play
recommended_minutes: 15-30
recommended_per_day: 1

Dogs benefit from at least one play or training session a day of 15 to 30 minutes, such as fetch, tug or puzzle toys. Mental enrichment reduces boredom and destructive chewing.

## Dog vet visits
species: dog
topic: vet
recommended_minutes: 60-90
recommended_per_day: 0

Healthy adult dogs should see a vet at least once a year for a checkup and vaccinations. Plan 60 to 90 minutes for a vet visit including travel. Senior dogs may need visits every six months.
