# General Pet Safety and Routine

## Consistent routines
species: any
topic: routine

Pets do best with a consistent daily routine. Feed, walk and play at similar times every day. Sudden changes in schedule can cause stress, so move task times gradually when possible.

## Water and heat safety
species: any
topic: safety

Always make fresh water available. Never leave a pet in a parked car. In hot weather, walk dogs early in the morning or late in the evening and avoid hot pavement.

## Toxic foods
species: any
topic: safety

Chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol sweetener and alcohol are toxic to dogs and cats. If a pet eats any of these, call a vet or poison control right away.

## Rabbits and other small pets
species: other
topic: feeding
recommended_minutes: 10-15
recommended_per_day: 2

Small pets such as rabbits need fresh hay available all day plus fresh vegetables once or twice a day. Cleaning the cage or hutch takes 10 to 15 minutes and should happen several times a week. Exotic pets have very specific needs, so check with a vet who treats them.
