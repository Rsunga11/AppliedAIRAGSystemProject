# General Pet Safety and Routine

## Consistent routines
species: any
topic: routine

Pets do best with a consistent daily routine. Feed, walk and play at similar times every day. Sudden changes in schedule can cause stress, so move task times gradually when possible.

## Water and heat safety
species: any
topic: safety

Always make fresh water available. Never leave a pet in a parked car. In hot weather, walk dogs early in the morning or late in the evening and avoid hot pavement.

## Toxic foods
species: any
topic: safety

Chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol sweetener and alcohol are toxic to dogs and cats. If a pet eats any of these, call a vet or poison control right away.

## Rabbit diet and daily routine

topic: feeding

Rabbits should have unlimited access to good-quality hay or grass and fresh water. The RSPCA describes a balanced rabbit diet as roughly 85% hay and grass, 10% leafy greens and 5% pellets or nuggets. Offer daily greens and pellets during the early morning and late afternoon, and avoid sudden diet changes. For scheduling only, allow 10 to 15 minutes for a routine feeding and water check; this is a planning estimate, not a dietary requirement. Ask a rabbit-savvy veterinarian before changing a rabbit's diet.
