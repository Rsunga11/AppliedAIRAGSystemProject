# Cat Care Guidelines

General guidance for adult indoor cats. Kittens, seniors and cats with medical conditions need individual advice from a vet.

## Cat feeding
species: cat
topic: feeding
recommended_minutes: 5-10
recommended_per_day: 2

Adult cats are usually fed two measured meals a day, about 10 to 12 hours apart, for example breakfast around 08:00 and dinner around 18:30. A feeding takes 5 to 10 minutes including fresh water. Some cats do well with small meals throughout the day. Kittens need three to four meals a day.

## Cat play and enrichment
species: cat
topic: play
recommended_minutes: 15-30
recommended_per_day: 2

Indoor cats need about 15 to 30 minutes of active play every day, ideally in two short sessions using wand toys or chasing games. Play prevents boredom and obesity, and it works best around dawn and dusk when cats are most active.

## Cat litter box care
species: cat
topic: litter
recommended_minutes: 5-10
recommended_per_day: 1

Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly. A scoop takes 5 to 10 minutes. A dirty box is a common reason cats stop using it.

## Cat grooming
species: cat
topic: grooming
recommended_minutes: 10-15
recommended_per_day: 0

Short-haired cats can be brushed weekly and long-haired cats several times a week. Grooming takes 10 to 15 minutes. Cats rarely need baths. Trim claws every few weeks.

## Cat vet visits
species: cat
topic: vet
recommended_minutes: 60-90
recommended_per_day: 0

Healthy adult cats should see a vet at least once a year for a checkup and vaccinations. Plan 60 to 90 minutes for a visit. Senior cats may need visits every six months.
