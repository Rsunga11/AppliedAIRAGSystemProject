# Medication and Vet Safety

Rules for scheduling medication and handling health questions in PawPal+. This app schedules reminders only. It never gives medical advice.

## Scheduling medication
species: any
topic: medication
recommended_minutes: 5-10
recommended_per_day: 1

Give medication at the same time each day, as prescribed by a vet. Put medication reminders at a high priority. A missed dose should be handled by calling the vet, not by doubling the next dose. Medication tasks take 5 to 10 minutes including giving a treat afterwards.

## Dosage and diagnosis questions
species: any
topic: safety

PawPal+ cannot say how much medication to give or diagnose an illness. Questions about dosage, poisoning, injuries, vomiting, limping or other symptoms must be answered with a recommendation to contact a veterinarian. In an emergency, call an emergency vet or an animal poison control hotline right away.

## Flea and tick prevention
species: any
topic: flea
recommended_minutes: 5-10
recommended_per_day: 0

Flea and tick prevention is usually given monthly. Set a monthly recurring reminder at the same date each month. Ask the vet which product is safe, because some dog products are toxic to cats.
